## 🎓 LESSONS FOR OTHER PRODUCTS

### Pattern 1: Feature Bloat
Subspace mixes OTT (shareable) + utilities (not shareable) in one product. Result: Confused positioning. Learning: Focus on single value prop until market proven. Expand later.

### Pattern 2: Network Effects
Group sharing has strong network effects (invite friends, build groups). But cold-start is killer. Learning: Pre-seed with influencers/curated content. Don't wait for organic network.

### Pattern 3: Metric Transparency
Subspace publishes no unit economics. Competitors (Splitwise CEO writes publicly) are transparent. Learning: Transparency builds trust, speeds fundraising, attracts talent.

### Pattern 4: Messaging Clarity
"Delivery in minutes" is wrong message for digital product. Learning: Test messaging with 50 users. If >20% misunderstand, it's broken.

### Pattern 5: Founder Focus
New CEO "AI-Native" pivot might be strategic, might be distraction. Learning: Focus on core until 5M users. Pivots come after market proven.
